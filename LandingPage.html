About

The CapCut AI Template Creator Programm

The Initiative

What it is ?

The CapCut AI Template Creator Program (ATCP) is an initiative designed for prompt artists and AI-native creators to turn their repeatable workflows into interactive templates that CapCut users can apply instantly.

Ideal Profile

Who We're Looking For ?

We’re looking for AI creators who love sharing their unique prompts, workflows, and short-form videos. If you’re passionate about your craft and want a platform that values your authorship, clear attribution, and monetizing your work, you’ll fit right in.

Creator Perks

What You Get ?

  • Financial Rewards: Start at $150 per week, with the potential to reach $250 per week as you advance to the Gold Tier. You can unlock this upgrade by maintaining 4 weeks of consistent work.
  • Professional Tools: Get a complimentary CapCut Pro membership and a steady supply of weekly AI credits to supercharge your workflow.
  • Official Recognition: Build your authority with an official CapCut Expert badge verified right on your creator profile.
  • Community & Networking: Receive exclusive invitations to official CapCut events and connect with a community of top-tier creators.
Expectations

What You Need to Deliver ?

  • Weekly Tasks: You are required to complete 2 tasks per week, resulting in a total of 20 AI videos.
  • Thematic Alignment: All videos must align with the specific themes provided for each task.
  • Video Format: Videos should be short-form (9:16 aspect ratio), typically lasting less than 10 seconds each.
  • Creative Focus: No advanced editing or technical production skills are required. The program is designed to leverage your unique AI creativity, prompt engineering, and workflow design.
